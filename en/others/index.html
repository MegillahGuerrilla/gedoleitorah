In their own writings

More Torah Leaders

What did other Torah leaders write on the same questions?

Torah leaders who are not in the two collections and who wrote on the same questions in their own books and letters. Their words are given as they wrote them, with the book and page. They are kept separate here because they are not part of either printed collection.

The sayings

  1. 1Lithuanian yeshiva heads

    In our days the Jews have chosen two idolatries to which they bring their offerings: socialism and nationalism. The teaching of the new nationalism can be defined in the fewest words: ‘Let us be like all the nations.’

    עברית · Hebrew original

    בימינו אלה בחרו להם היהודים בשתי עבודות זרות אשר להן יקריבו את קרבנותיהם, הרי הם: הסוציאליזם והנציונליזם [הלאומיות]. את תורת הנציונליזם החדש אפשר להגדיר בקצור נמרץ: ׳נהיה ככל הגויים׳.

    Rabbi Elchanan Bunim WassermanRosh yeshiva of Ohel Torah, Baranovich

    Ikvesa DeMeshicha, section 21. Written in Yiddish; Hebrew translation by Rabbi Moshe Scheinfeld, Netzach, Bnei Brak 5749 (1989), pp. 35–36

The sayings are quoted from the printed books. The English translations were made for this site.
