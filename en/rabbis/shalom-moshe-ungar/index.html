Rabbi Shalom Moshe HaLevi Ungar

Chief Rabbi and Rosh Yeshiva of Nitra

Died 2003. Reopened the Nitra yeshiva after the war and moved it to the US (Mount Kisco).

Places: Born in Krompachy · Served in Nitra, Mount Kisco, NY

Those who continue the chain of tradition of our holy forefathers from generation to generation absolutely oppose and reject every form of association and partnership with heretics and deniers.

עברית · Hebrew original

הממשיכים שלשלת מסורת אבותינו הקדושים מדור דור, מתנגדים ופוסלים בהחלט כל אופן של התחברות ושותפות עם מינים וכופרים.

Menachem Av 5737 (1977) · Knesset Elections, 39
