Rabbi Moshe Teitelbaum

Satmar Rebbe, author of Berach Moshe

1914–2006. Rabbi of Sighet, then the Sigheter Rebbe in Williamsburg from 1947, and Satmar Rebbe from 1980. Author of Berach Moshe.

Places: Born in Újfehértó · Served in Senta, Sighet, Williamsburg, Brooklyn

…Each and every person must warn his household, his friends and his acquaintances not to desecrate G-d's name in public, and not to stumble, Heaven forbid, by taking part in the forbidden elections.

עברית · Hebrew original

...ועל כל אחד ואחד להזהיר את ב״ב ואת אוהביו ומכיריו לא לחלל ש״ש ברבים ולא להכשל ח״ו בהשתתפות בבחירות האסורות.

Tammuz 5711 (1951) · Knesset Elections, 2
