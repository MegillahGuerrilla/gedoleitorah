Rabbi Mordechai Chaim of Slonim

Slonimer Rebbe

1868–1953. Led the Slonim Hasidim in Jerusalem after the Holocaust.

Places: Served in Jerusalem

…Whoever takes part and has a hand in these forbidden elections should know that he is staking his own life and the lives of his children and household.

...כל מי שישתתף ויקח חבל בבחירות אסורות אלו, ידע כי בנפשו ונפש בניו ובני ביתו הוא קובע.

Tammuz 5711 (1951) · Knesset Elections, 39
