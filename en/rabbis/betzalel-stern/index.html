Rabbi Betzalel Stern

Rabbi in Vienna; author of Responsa B'Tzel HaChochma

1911–1988. Rabbi in Sibiu, then of Adass Israel in Melbourne (1954) and Kehal Yereim in Vienna (1968); settled in Jerusalem in 1982. Died on election day, November 1, 1988.

Places: Served in Sibiu, Melbourne, Vienna, Jerusalem

There is no permission whatsoever under Torah law to join this regime — even if the government were composed entirely of Torah- and mitzvah-observant Jews.

עברית · Hebrew original

אין שום היתר על פי התורה להצטרף לשלטון זה, אף אם היתה ממשלה כולה מורכבת מיהודים שומרי תורה ומצות.

Elul 5737 (1977) · Knesset Elections, 24
