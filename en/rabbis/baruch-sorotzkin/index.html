Rabbi Baruch Sorotzkin

1917–1979

From the book (1980)

Rosh yeshiva of Telshe in Cleveland and one of the leaders of the Moetzes Gedolei HaTorah in America. One of the great Torah teachers of our generation. Son of Rabbi Zalman Sorotzkin, chairman of the Moetzes Gedolei HaTorah in the Land of Israel.

Places: Served in Cleveland

The talk of “peace” is false and misleading.
