Rabbi Moshe Yitzchok Gewirtzman

Pshevorsker Rebbe

1881–1976 ('Reb Itzikl' of Pshevorsk). Exiled to Siberia, later in Paris; settled in Antwerp in 1956. Died on Yom Kippur.

Places: Born in Gorlice · Served in Przeworsk, Paris, Antwerp

…Not to go to the elections, so as not to transgress, Heaven forbid, the severe prohibitions that branch out from it.

עברית · Hebrew original

...לבל ילכו להבחירות שלא לעבור ח״ו על האיסורים החמורים המסתעפים מזה.

Elul 5719 (1959) · Knesset Elections, 11
