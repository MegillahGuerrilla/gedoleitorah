Rabbi Yaakov Betzalel Zolty

1920–1982

From the book (1980)

Rabbi and head of the rabbinical court of Jerusalem and a member of the Great Rabbinical Court. Author of Mishnat Yaavetz on Orach Chaim, Yoreh De'ah and Choshen Mishpat. One of the great teachers of Torah and leading rabbis in Israel.

Places: Served in Jerusalem

Holding the territories is not a political question but a halachic one.
