Rabbi Chaim Zanvil Abramowitz

Ribnitzer Rebbe

Died 1995 in Monsey. The Ribnitzer Rebbe.

Places: Born in Botoșani · Served in Rîbnița, Monsey

…Even if it is as they claim, that they obtained some promises for the benefit of religion, there is no permission whatsoever under Torah law to join this regime.
