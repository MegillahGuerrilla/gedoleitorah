Rabbi Zelig Reuven Bengis

Chief Rabbi of Jerusalem (Edah HaChareidis)

1864–1953. Rabbi of Kalvarija in Lithuania; head of the Edah HaChareidis court from 1948. Author of Leflagos Reuven.

Places: Born in Vilnius · Served in Boćki, Kalvarija, Jerusalem

…It is forbidden for any Jew to take part in their elections.

עברית · Hebrew original

...אסור לכל בר ישראל להשתתף לבחירות שלהם.

Tammuz 5711 (1951) · Knesset Elections, 3
