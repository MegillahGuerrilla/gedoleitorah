Rabbi Moshe Stern

Chief Rabbi of Debrecen; author of Responsa Be'er Moshe

1914–1997. Judge in Debrecen, later rav of Kahal Yesodei HaTorah in New York. Author of Responsa Be'er Moshe.

Places: Born in Nové Zámky · Served in Debrecen, Buenos Aires, New York

They bear responsibility for everything the government of heretics decides and carries out, and the yoke of guilt hangs on their necks.

עברית · Hebrew original

הם נושאים באחריות בכל מה שמחלטת ומבצעת ממשלת הכופרים, והקולר תלוי בצוארם.

Menachem Av 5737 (1977) · Knesset Elections, 12
