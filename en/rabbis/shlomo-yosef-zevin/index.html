Giving Up Land Hasidic courts

Rabbi Shlomo Yosef Zevin

1888–1978

From the book (1980)

One of the leading Torah giants of the generation and editor of the Encyclopedia Talmudit. A close disciple of the authors of Aruch HaShulchan and Shemen LaMaor, who ordained him. He corresponded on halacha with Rabbi Avraham Menachem Steinberg of Brody and Rabbi Shimon Shkop. Author of many books, renowned for his brilliance and singular greatness.

Places: Served in Jerusalem

Defending the territories of the Land of Israel is a milchemet mitzvah, a commanded war.

עברית · Hebrew original

ההגנה על שטחי ארץ ישראל – מלחמת מצוה

Returning the territories is a grave danger, Heaven forbid, to the survival of the people and the land.
