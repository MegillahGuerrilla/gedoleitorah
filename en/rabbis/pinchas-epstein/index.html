Knesset Elections The Edah HaChareidis of Jerusalem

Rabbi Pinchas Epstein

Head of the Jerusalem Rabbinical Court (Edah HaChareidis)

Member of the Edah HaChareidis court. The Hamichlol encyclopedia reports that the Chazon Ish sent word to strengthen his refusal to take part in elections.

Places: Served in Jerusalem

Whoever takes part in these elections lends a hand to transgressors, and he must answer for the consequences of his taking part in the house of heresy that reviles and blasphemes the armies of the living G-d.

עברית · Hebrew original

המשתתף בבחירות אלו נותן יד לפושעים ועליו לדאוג מאחריות תוצאות השתתפותו בבית המינות המחרף ומגדף מערכות אלקים חיים.

Tammuz 5721 (1961) · Knesset Elections, 9
