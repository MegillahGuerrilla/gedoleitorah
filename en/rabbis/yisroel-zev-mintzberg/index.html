Rabbi Yisroel Zev Mintzberg

Rabbinical judge in the Holy City; author of Responsa She'eris Yisroel

Jerusalem rabbinical judge of the Edah HaChareidis; author of Responsa She'eris Yisroel.

Places: Served in Jerusalem

I explained the view of the holy Torah in my book Zos Chukas HaTorah: in any public matter that is conducted, in error, against the laws of the holy Torah, the prohibition applies also to the individuals who took part in it.

עברית · Hebrew original

ביארתי דעת תוה״ק בס' ״זאת חוקת התורה״ שבכל ענין ציבורי שבמשגה נעשה בניגוד לחוקי תוה״ק חל איסור גם על היחידים שהשתתפו לזה.

10 Kislev 5709 (December 1948) · Knesset Elections, 37
