Knesset Elections The Edah HaChareidis of Jerusalem

Rabbi Yisroel Yitzchok Reisman

Head of the Jerusalem Rabbinical Court (Edah HaChareidis)

1889–1969. Judge on the Edah HaChareidis court from 1942; helped found Yeshivat Sfas Emes in Jerusalem.

Places: Served in Jerusalem

It is an absolute prohibition for every Jew, man or woman, young or old, to take any part in such elections; and whoever heeds will dwell secure and at ease.

עברית · Hebrew original

איסור גמור לכל בר ישראל, איש או אשה, קטן וגדול לקחת חבל בבחירות כאלה, והשומע ישכון בטח ושאנן.

26 Sivan 5715 (1955) · Knesset Elections, 7
