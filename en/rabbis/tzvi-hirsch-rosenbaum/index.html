Rabbi Tzvi Hirsch Rosenbaum

Kretshnif Rebbe

1920–2006. Born in Sighet; rebuilt Kretshnif Hasidism in Israel (Kiryat Ata, Rehovot, Kiryat Gat, Bnei Brak, Jerusalem). Close to the Satmar Rav.

Places: Born in Sighet · Served in Jerusalem

…From "In the beginning G-d created" to "before the eyes of all Israel" [i.e., in the entire Torah], there is no prohibition like going to the elections… for it has already become clear that the main aim of the heretics is to uproot the Torah and to make the House of Israel like all the nations, Heaven help us.

עברית · Hebrew original

...מבראשית ברא עד לעיני כל ישראל אין עוד איסור כהליכה לבחירות... כי כבר נתברר שעיקר כוונתם של הכופרים לעקור התורה וככל הגוים ב״י רח״ל.

From a sermon, 21 Sivan 5752 (1992) · Knesset Elections, 1
