Rabbi Mordechai Sawitzki

From the book (1980)

Head of the rabbinical court of Boston. One of the leading Torah giants of our generation. Author of Mayim Adirim, Mar'eh Esh and Berechat Yerushalayim on the Babylonian and Jerusalem Talmuds, and Berechat HaMelech on Maimonides' count of the commandments. A close disciple of the Chafetz Chaim and of the Rogatchover Gaon, whose responsa to him he collected in a separate volume, Ner Avraham.

Places: Served in Boston

Returning territories will endanger the land and those who live in it.
