Knesset Elections The Edah HaChareidis of Jerusalem

Rabbi Yisroel Moshe Dushinsky

Chief Rabbi of Jerusalem (Edah HaChareidis)

1921–2003. Second Dushinsky Rebbe; on the Edah HaChareidis court from 1967 and its head 1996–2003.

Places: Born in Khust · Served in Jerusalem

We have already faithfully made known the view of the Torah regarding the elections to the regime's Knesset: it is forbidden to take part in elections to the Knesset in the Holy Land.

עברית · Hebrew original

כבר הודענו נאמנה דע״ת בנידון הבחירות לכנסת השלטון כי אסור להשתתף בבחירות לכנסת בארה״ק.

Shevat 5763 (2003) · Knesset Elections, 6
