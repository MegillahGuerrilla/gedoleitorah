Rabbi Avraham Dovid Horowitz

Member of the Badatz; head of the Strasbourg rabbinical court; author of Kinyan Torah

1911–2004. Rabbinical judge and head of the court in Strasbourg from 1947; moved to Jerusalem in 1978 and joined the Edah HaChareidis court in 1980. Author of Kinyan Torah.

Places: Born in Bolechów · Served in Oradea (Grosswardein), Strasbourg, Jerusalem

…He must answer for the consequences of his taking part in the house of heretics that reviles and blasphemes the armies of the living G-d and wages war against our Written and Oral Torah.

עברית · Hebrew original

...עליו לדאוג מאחריות תוצאות השתתפותו בבית המינים המחרף ומגדף מערכות אלוקים חיים ולוחם נגד תורתינו הכתובה והמסורה.

Cheshvan 5721 (1960) · Knesset Elections, 4
