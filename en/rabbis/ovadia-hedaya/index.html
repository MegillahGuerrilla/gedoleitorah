Giving Up Land Sephardi and Mizrahi

Rabbi Ovadia Hedaya

From the book (1980)

Foremost among the Sephardi Torah giants of his generation. A member of the rabbinical court of the Sephardi community in Jerusalem and later of the Great Rabbinical Court of Appeals. Author of the responsa Yaskil Avdi and of Deah VeHaskel on Kabbalah. He was revered and accepted by rabbis of every circle, among them the kabbalist Rabbi David Jungreis, head of the Edah HaChareidis court in Jerusalem, who corresponded with him extensively on halacha.
