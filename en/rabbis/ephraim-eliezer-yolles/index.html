Rabbi Ephraim Eliezer HaKohen Yolles

From the book (1980)

Head of the rabbinical court of Philadelphia and honorary president of the Agudath HaRabbonim of the United States and Canada. Descended from generation after generation of renowned halachic authorities, he is one of the leading decisors of halacha in America and author of Ketzeh HaHar.

Places: Served in Philadelphia

Every individual and every community has an absolute duty to act to prevent the return of the territories.
