Rabbi Pinchas Hirschprung

1912–1998

From the book (1980)

Head of the rabbinical court of Montreal and Chief Rabbi of Canadian Jewry. One of the last of the prodigies of Poland and a close disciple of the Polish Torah giants who ordained him. One of the most encyclopedic scholars of our generation. Considered one of the leading rabbis of Canada and the United States, he heads many Torah institutions and projects.

Places: Born in Dukla · Served in Montreal

Saving lives (pikuach nefesh) overrides the return of territories.
