Rabbi Yosef S. HaKohen

Elder of the rabbis of Tunis

1891–1980. Hacham Yosef Soso HaKohen, associate chief rabbi and head of the rabbinical court of Tunis; in Jerusalem, a judge of the Sephardi Edah HaChareidis.

Places: Born in Gabès · Served in Tunis, Beersheba, Jerusalem

…There is no power in the world that can permit it in any way whatsoever; this terrible prohibition stands in full force.

עברית · Hebrew original

...ואין שום כח בעולם אשר יתיר באיזה אופן שהוא תוקף האיסור הנורא הזה.

10 Tammuz 5737 (1977) · Knesset Elections, 25
