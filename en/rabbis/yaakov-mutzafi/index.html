Knesset Elections Sephardi and Mizrahi

Rabbi Yaakov Mutzafi

Chief Rabbi of the Sephardic Edah HaChareidis

1900–1983. Kabbalist from Baghdad; head of the Sephardi Edah HaChareidis court; close to the Satmar Rebbe and strongly opposed to voting.

Places: Born in Baghdad · Served in Jerusalem

Whoever takes part in these elections breaches the fence. It is as though he acknowledges the laws of the State, which are against the laws of the Torah, and he gives strength to the Other Side and to those who blaspheme G-d's name.

עברית · Hebrew original

כל הנוטל חלק בבחירות אלו פורץ גדר הוא, והרי זה כמודה בחוקי המדינה שהם כנגד חוקי התורה, ונותן כח להסט״א ומנאצים שם הוי״ה ית״ש.

14 Tammuz 5711 (1951) · Knesset Elections, 19
