Giving Up Land Sephardi and Mizrahi

Rishon LeZion Rabbi Ovadia Yosef

1920–2013

From the book (1980)

Chief Rabbi of Israel, Rishon LeZion. Author of Yabia Omer, six volumes on all sections of the Shulchan Aruch. One of the leading rabbis of our generation.

Places: Born in Baghdad · Served in Jerusalem

Note Rav Ovadia's ruling turns on pikuach nefesh. In his well-known 1979 ruling he held that the decision depends on the judgment of security experts: where giving up territory endangers lives, it is forbidden.

Halacha forbids returning territories wherever lives are at stake.
