The Klausenburger Rebbe, Rabbi Yekusiel Yehuda Halberstam

1905–1994

From the book (1980)

A towering Torah genius and one of the greatest Hasidic rebbes of our generation. After the terrible Holocaust in Europe he raised up the yoke of Torah again, founding a network of Torah institutions in the Holy Land and the United States. His halachic lectures won renown among Torah scholars of every background. The words quoted here were spoken before thousands of Jews in the holy city of Jerusalem during his visit there in the summer of 5738 (1978).

Places: Served in Cluj (Klausenburg), Williamsburg, Brooklyn, Netanya (Kiryat Sanz)

The Land of Israel will be ours for as long as the world exists.

עברית · Hebrew original

ארץ ישראל תהא שלנו כל זמן שהעולם קיים
