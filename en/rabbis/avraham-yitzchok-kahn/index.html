Rabbi Avraham Yitzchok Kahn

Toldos Aharon Rebbe

1914–1996. The Toldos Aharon Rebbe from 1947; forbade voting and army service. Author of Divrei Emuna.

Places: Born in Safed · Served in Jerusalem

…All the voters become partners, through their representatives sitting in the Knesset, in acknowledging idolatry and in the severe prohibitions.

עברית · Hebrew original

...כל הבוחרים נעשים שותפים באמצעות הודאה בע״ז ובאיסורים החמורים, באמצעות נציגיהם באי כוחם היושבים בכנסת.

Cheshvan 5749 (1988) · Knesset Elections, 31
