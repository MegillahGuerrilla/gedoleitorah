Rabbi Shmuel Tzvi Horowitz

Spinka Rebbe

Died 1997. The Spinka Rebbe in Williamsburg; close to the Satmar Rebbe.

Places: Served in Williamsburg, Brooklyn

…The very act of taking part in and sitting in the Knesset of heretics is a full acknowledgment of the kingdom of heresy, which rebels against the Kingdom of Heaven.

עברית · Hebrew original

...עצם ההשתתפות והישיבה בכנסת המינים, היא הודאה גמורה במלכות המינות המורדת במלכות שמים.

Cheshvan 5749 (1988) · Knesset Elections, 32
