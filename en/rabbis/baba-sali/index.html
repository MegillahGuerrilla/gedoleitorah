Rabbi Yisrael Abuhatzeira

The Baba Sali

1889–1984. The Baba Sali of Netivot.

Places: Born in Rissani · Served in Lod, Netivot

I hereby join my opinion to theirs — the view of the Torah that they ruled — that it is forbidden to take part in the elections of the regime, for it is against our holy Torah…

עברית · Hebrew original

הנני מצרף דעתי אל דעתם דעת תורה שפסקו שאסור להשתתף בבחירות של השלטון אשר זה נגד תורתינו הקדושה...

Rosh Chodesh Adar I 5725 (1965) · Knesset Elections, 21 · The source of this statement is being verified.
