Giving Up Land Sephardi and Mizrahi

Rishon LeZion Rabbi Yitzhak Nissim

1896–1981

From the book (1980)

Rishon LeZion and former Chief Rabbi of the Land of Israel. Author of Yayin HaTov and Kenogah Tzedakah. He published the responsa of the great Rabbi Shlomo Eliezer Alfandari (the Maharsha Alfandari).

Places: Born in Baghdad · Served in Jerusalem

The government has no authority to return even a single foot of land.

עברית · Hebrew original

אין רשות לממשלה להחזיר אף שעל אדמה אחד
