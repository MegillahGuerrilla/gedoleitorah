Rabbi Chaim Sinwani

Chief Rabbi of Sharab, Yemen

1897–1979. Yemenite kabbalist and judge; rabbi of Sinwan, on the Aden court; came to Israel in 1949 and lived in Yehud. Author of Arzei HaLevanon.

Places: Born in Sinwan (Sharab) · Served in Aden, Yehud

…It is forbidden to enter a house where words of heresy and denial are heard, and likewise it is forbidden to send emissaries there, for a person's agent is as himself.

עברית · Hebrew original

...אסור להכנס לבית שנשמעים שם דברי מינות וכפירה, וכן אסור לשלוח שם שליחים, כי שליחו של אדם כמותו.

Iyar 5737 (1977) · Knesset Elections, 13
