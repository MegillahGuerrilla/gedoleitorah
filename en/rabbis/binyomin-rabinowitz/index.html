Rabbi Binyomin Rabinowitz

Member of the Badatz; Rebbe of Mishkenos HaRo'im

1912–2002. Born in Jerusalem's Old City; rosh yeshiva at Toldos Aharon, judge on the Edah HaChareidis court from 1976, founder of Mishkenos HaRo'im.

Places: Born in Jerusalem · Served in Jerusalem

Already at the first elections to the Knesset they ruled it a severe prohibition, and a ruling founded on the eternal holy Torah — no one has the power to annul it, Heaven forbid.

עברית · Hebrew original

כבר בבחירות הראשונות להכנסת הורו לאיסור חמור, והפסק דין שיסודו על פי תוה״ק הנצחית אין בכח מי שהוא לבטלו ח״ו.

Iyar 5737 (1977) · Knesset Elections, 8
