Rabbi Azriel Yehuda Lebowitz

Head of the Vien (Vienna) rabbinical court

Died 1991. Rav of Kehal Adas Yereim Vien in Brooklyn from 1958; founder of Mesivta Nachlas Yakov; author of Ezer MiYehuda.

Places: Served in Hodász, Williamsburg, Brooklyn

…By the very act of taking part in the Knesset of heretics there is consent to the rule of the heretics and to the laws of wickedness and transgression they have enacted.

עברית · Hebrew original

...בעצם השתתפותם בכנסת המינים יש משום הסכמה לשלטון הכופרים ולחוקי הרשע והפשע שחקקו.

Av 5737 (1977) · Knesset Elections, 16
