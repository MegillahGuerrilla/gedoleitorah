Giving Up Land Israel’s rabbinical courts and Chief Rabbinate

Rabbi Yehoshua Menachem Ehrenberg

1904–1976

From the book (1980)

Head of the rabbinical court of Tel Aviv–Jaffa and author of the monumental Devar Yehoshua on all four sections of the Shulchan Aruch. One of the last of the great Torah scholars of Galicia and a leading disciple of the gaon of his generation, Rabbi Meir Arik, author of Imrei Yosher. A leading Belz Hasid, very close to the Belzer Rebbe.

Places: Served in Tel Aviv–Jaffa

The view of the halachic authorities is clear: it is forbidden to return territories.
