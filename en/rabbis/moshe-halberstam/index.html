Knesset Elections The Edah HaChareidis of Jerusalem

Rabbi Moshe Halberstam

Member of the Badatz; author of Responsa Divrei Moshe

1932–2006. Rosh yeshiva of Tshakava; on the Edah HaChareidis court from 1997. Author of Divrei Moshe.

Places: Born in Szczakowa · Served in Jerusalem

…It is forbidden to take part in the elections in the Holy Land, and of this it is said: 'The word of our G-d shall stand forever.'

עברית · Hebrew original

...אסור להשתתף בבחירות בארה״ק וע״ז נאמר ודבר אלוקינו יקום לעולם.

Adar 5766 (2006) · Knesset Elections, 41
