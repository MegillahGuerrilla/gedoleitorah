Rabbi Dovid Jungreis

Head of the Jerusalem Rabbinical Court (Edah HaChareidis)

Born 1898. Head of the Edah HaChareidis court from 1968 until his death.

Places: Served in Jerusalem

…He must answer for the consequences of his taking part in the house of heretics that reviles and blasphemes the armies of the living G-d.
