Giving Up Land Hasidic courts

Rabbi Nachum Trebnik

From the book (1980)

Rabbi and head of the rabbinical court of Kfar Chabad, one of the heads of the Chabad rabbinical court in Israel and of the Tomchei Temimim yeshivas. Son-in-law of Rabbi Shneur Zalman Garelik, head of the Kfar Chabad court, whom he succeeded after his passing. Considered one of the leading rabbis in Israel.

Places: Served in Kfar Chabad

It is forbidden to bring our enemies closer to Israel's border.
