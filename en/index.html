How to read this site

Each collection stands on its own. It brings the words of the rabbis who answered one question and attributes to them no position on the other. No rabbi appears in both collections.

Each collection shows who stood together: rabbis of different communities and circles, from Jerusalem to the Diaspora, who reached the same answer.
