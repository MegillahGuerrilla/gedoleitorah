1937

The Peel Commission proposes partitioning the Land; the Third Knessia Gedola meets in Marienbad

From the Council of Torah Sages, 5697 (1937)The Council of Torah Sages declares that the borders of our Holy Land were set by the One who apportions the lands, in His holy Torah, and are fixed for all generations. It is therefore impossible for the Jewish people, for its part, to give up these borders in any form whatsoever. Any such concession has no validity at all.

1979

The peace treaty with Egypt (March)

All this time I have refrained from directing my words on this matter to any particular movement, not even to our own chasidim and the like. I stress explicitly the opposite: this is a matter that concerns every single Jew… Even when we were exiled and removed from it, it is our country and our land… the Land, which is in the full and eternal ownership of the people of Israel… every single one is not only permitted to state his opinion regarding our land, the land of our fathers, but is also obligated to do so, with all due force.The Lubavitcher Rebbe

The two questions

Each collection stands on its own. It brings the words of the rabbis who answered one question and attributes to them no position on the other. No rabbi appears in both collections.

Each collection shows who stood together: rabbis of different communities and circles, from Jerusalem to the Diaspora, who reached the same answer.

The two questions were answered by different rabbis, and those rabbis did not share one view of the state. What joins them? Among the rabbis who forbade giving up land, some wrote plainly that the ruling does not depend on one’s view of the state, because it rests on danger to life. And by the ruling of the rabbis of the second collection, the voter who sends a representative to the Knesset is a partner in what it decides about the Land.
